What a quality link means here

Local

Frederick and Hagerstown news, chamber of commerce, sponsorships, community organizations. The category you have none of.

Industry

Manufacturer features, trade publications, roofing and home-improvement sites. The ones Shumaker already has.

Digital PR

One data-driven story a quarter, pitched to regional press. What a roof really costs in Washington County in 2026 is a story a local paper will run.

Never

Directory farms, private blog networks, bulk "packages," anything with a .shop in the name. The bar: would we be comfortable if you saw where it came from?
