08 · The link program

SIX REAL LINKS A MONTH,
DONE RIGHT.

Two ways to get a link: earn it or buy it. We do both, and we're clear about which is which.

Month 1

Clean the slate

Full audit of all 485 domains. Disavow file for the spam. Monitoring in place, because the network is adding three a day and it'll keep adding them. First prospect list built and shared with you.

Month 2

Strategy and prospecting

Target authority range, relevance bar, link-type mix and per-link budget locked. 30 to 50 prospect domains scored on authority, relevance, traffic and ease. Every target page on your site gets a share. First links land.

Month 3 onward

Six links a month, every month

Distributed across the cost pages and the Frederick and Hagerstown hubs by keyword value. Every outcome logged in a central link database you can see at any time: date, source, authority, traffic, and which page it points at.

What a quality link means here

Local

Hagerstown and Frederick news, chamber of commerce, sponsorships, community organizations. The category you have none of.

Industry

Manufacturer features, trade publications, roofing and home-improvement sites. The ones Shumaker already has.

Digital PR

One data-driven story a quarter, pitched to regional press. What a roof really costs in Washington County in 2026 is a story a local paper will run.

Never

Directory farms, private blog networks, bulk "packages," anything with a .shop in the name. The bar: would we be comfortable if you saw where it came from?

The placement cost is the placement cost. It's itemized on your invoice. Unused budget doesn't get spent to hit a number.

The accelerant, not the engine

Google discounts some paid links. That's fine. The earned links are the core; paid ones get the flywheel moving faster.
