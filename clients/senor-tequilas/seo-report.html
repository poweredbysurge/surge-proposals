06 · The local stack

For a restaurant,
the map is the battlefield

Every money keyword shows a local pack, so half the engagement runs on the local stack via BrightLocal:

Citation Builder + NAP cleanupAcross aggregators and directories. This also converts the junk-directory backlink profile into a consistent, verified one.
Local rank grid trackingWeekly geo-grid scans for "mexican restaurant," "tacos," "margaritas," "happy hour," and "catering" across the Germantown, Gaithersburg, and Clarksburg map. The metric that correlates with walk-ins.
Review managementMonitoring and response across Google, Yelp, and TripAdvisor. Already leads on volume (4.1★ / 301); the play is velocity (weekly fresh reviews) and lift toward 4.4★+.
GBP as a publishing channelWeekly posts, Q&A seeding, photo cadence.

Also flagged: the brand-name collision with unrelated "Señor Tequila" restaurants in other states leaks branded search. Consistent NAP, schema, and a locally-anchored site resolve the entity for Google. A quiet month-1 win.
