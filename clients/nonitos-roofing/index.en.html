04 · How It Brings In Work

THE PAGES BRING THEM IN.
THE FORMS TURN THEM INTO JOBS.

A good-looking website that nobody finds does not pay for itself. Here is how ours goes from a Google search to a lead on your phone.

01

A homeowner searches

They type something like "roof repair" and the name of their town. Google looks for a page that matches that exact service in that exact place.

02

They land on a page written for that search

They do not land on a generic home page. They land on a page about their problem, in their town, with your photos and your reviews on it.

03

The form asks for just enough

Name, phone, zip code, and what they need. Short forms get filled out. Long ones get abandoned. The form and a call button are on every page.

04

You hear about it right away

The lead goes to your phone and email the moment the form is sent. It is also saved in your Surge Command Center, the dashboard where you see every lead and where it came from, so nothing gets lost in a text thread.

Most roofing websites are a home page, a services list, and a contact page. That gives Google three pages to work with. Yours launches with a page for every service and every city you care about, and we keep adding more every month.
